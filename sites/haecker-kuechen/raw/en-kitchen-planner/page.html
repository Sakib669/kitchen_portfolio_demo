Online kitchen planner – plan your kitchen in 3D from the comfort of your own home

Design your new kitchen to suit your individual needs. With Häcker's free 3D kitchen planner, you can bring your ideas to life and immediately see what your kitchen design looks like as a realistic model.

Which front do you prefer? Do you prefer handle-free? Where would you like to place attractive cabinets? Plan your kitchen according to your own ideas or be inspired by our kitchen ideas. You can save your designs at any time and develop them further later or refine them together with a specialist dealer.

Plan your dream kitchen yourself now, quickly and free of charge!

Directly to the kitchen planner

Step by step to your own kitchen design

Step 1: Collect wishes and record basic ideas

Before you start planning, it is worth taking a look at your personal needs. Think about how you use your kitchen on a daily basis, what styles you like and which functions are particularly important to you. These initial thoughts will help you to establish a clear framework for your new kitchen.

Step 2: Measure the room and enter the dimensions

Carefully measure the wall lengths, room height and existing connections. The more accurate your measurements, the more realistic the subsequent 3D representation of your kitchen will be.

Step 3: Create a floor plan in the kitchen planner

Create your room in the kitchen planner: position windows, doors and radiators, select wall colours and floor coverings, and design the floor plan to reflect the actual layout of the room.

Directly to the kitchen planner

Step 4: Plan your kitchen freely

 

Choose from numerous Häcker fronts, cabinet types, worktops and electrical appliances. Place elements freely in the room and adapt your plan step by step to your wishes.

 

Step 5: Save up to three designs or go directly to the retailer

 

Save up to three different kitchen designs and compare them at your leisure. You can reopen your plan at any time, develop it further, print it out or finalise it together with a Häcker specialist retailer.

You can benefit from this with Häcker's kitchen planner.

With the Häcker kitchen planner, you can design your kitchen intuitively. Whether you want a U-shape, L-shape, cooking island or compact kitchenette – the tool shows you every option realistically in 3D and helps you quickly get a coherent overall impression.

Questions about individual kitchen planning

How do I begin planning my kitchen?

Start by measuring the available space and entering the dimensions into the kitchen planner. You can then design the floor plan and begin the actual planning process.

Is the kitchen planner really free of charge?

Yes, the Häcker kitchen planner is completely free of charge and can be used immediately without registration.

Can I save multiple drafts?

You can save up to three plans, compare them and, if necessary, retrieve or edit them again.

Can I discuss my plans with a dealer?

Yes. Your saved designs can be printed out or taken with you to a meeting with a Häcker specialist dealer, who will professionally review and refine your plans.

On which devices does the kitchen planner work?

The kitchen planner runs in your browser on your PC, tablet or smartphone – no download or installation required.

Be your own kitchen planner and design your dream kitchen in just a few steps – our planning tool makes it possible.
