Kitchen styles at Häcker: your perfect match!

Häcker kitchen styles offer unbound creativity potential. Unlimited design freedom, customisable dimensions and modern technology make our kitchen solutions your perfect match. Uncompromisingly excellent workmanship – made in Germany – meets exclusive aesthetics. Uniform design and attractive details make our kitchens the perfectly equipped hub for your daily life. Whether as a venue for cooking parties among friends or as a family meeting place, your Häcker kitchen adapts to your needs and lifestyle.

Our all-rounder systemat

Looking for something truly out of the ordinary? Our systemat product line creates compact room concepts with smart architecture and unusual materials. Whether dark grey stone veneer, noble Calacatta ceramic or high-quality real wood grooved front, our systemat product line dazzles with a huge selection of sizes and materials. Conceive your desired design from 66 different surfaces and create a timeless composition. Whether pantry or open plan, systemat produces perfectly designed kitchens that add class and style to your cooking area. Our easy-care PerfectSense® surfaces captivate users not only with their wide range of colours and velvety feel but also with their low maintenance. Thanks to floating shelves, modern sideboards and technical refinements, the clearly structured ambience that emerges is superbly staged with integrated lighting elements. Lofts, tiny houses, apartments and holiday homes become real design gems.

The smart kitchen solution: concept130

Our product line concept130 offers nearly endless design options thanks to numerous choices and combinations. From warm walnut to monochrome black to Scandinavian white tones: with concept130, sophisticated functionality meets impressive aesthetics. Do you love the country style with its homely, relaxed ambience? With our KORFU, CAPRI, TOSCANA, BRISTOL and HAMPTON country-style lines, you can choose among five beautiful coffered fronts ranging from modern to traditional. Or do you prefer urban style? If so, we recommend our handleless ART series, whose wide, smooth surfaces look timelessly elegant and cleanly modern at the same time. Be it a plain PERFECT SOFT in wonderfully elegant black or our high-gloss FARO in clean crystal white or muted cashmere. Whether industrial style, wood and earthy natural look or a deeply coloured kitchen, our product line offers a wide range of options to make your individual kitchen dream come true. Because your wishes are our driving force. Resistant materials and high-quality lacquers make your kitchen a real statement. 

Kitchens of unlimited functionality: Häcker technology

Häcker leaves nothing to chance to ensure maximum functionality. This also applies, for example, to the quality of our edges. PUResist is our innovative gluing technique, whereby our edges become water-repellent and extremely resistant to external influences. And all this without the use of substances containing formaldehyde or solvents. Not surprisingly, this yields a perfect look and outstanding quality in all high-wear kitchen spots. When it comes to worktops, we also attach great importance to their optimum quality for everyday use. Our PerfectSense® coating, available in many ranges, has a special micro-surface structure that immediately releases hand perspiration back into the ambient air. In other words, fingerprints hardly stick to this high-quality lacquer coating. 

 

Comfort meets minimalism

We also place great emphasis on convenience in kitchen handling. For handleless kitchens, therefore, Tipmatic Softclose is available along with push-to-open – a spring-loaded lock. This mechanism allows pull-outs and drawers to slide smoothly to their full opening position when light pressure is applied. For those who prefer beautiful kitchen fronts to built-in electrical appliances, we recommend our Pocket Doors. Attached to the side of the tall cabinet, they can be fully retracted into recesses on the side during cooking and baking and then extended and folded shut again after cooking. This provides maximum flexibility and the option to tidy up your cabinet unit with just one movement, at any time. By the way, we also make no compromises when it comes to lighting, as light will usually make or break an inviting ambience. That's why we offer numerous integrated lighting options, whether in showcases, pull-outs, shelves or cabinets. For an exclusive balance of handling and aesthetics.

Accessories for your kitchen style at Häcker

Tidy house, tidy mind.. or so the saying goes. Round  off your kitchen with accessories that are perfectly matched to your kitchen. This starts with our flexible plug-in systems for your pull-outs and high-quality cutlery inserts for drawers and comprises flexible solutions for individual table settings. Technical finesse from Häcker

Good technology can be recognised by its practical applications. Thus, Häcker consistently strives to find innovative solutions to simplify your daily life. Efficient storage space solutions are paramount here. And when it comes to storage, our tandem swing pull-out sets a high mark: as you open the door, not only does the cabinet shelf move towards you but the door itself offers extra storage through an additional shelf. This way, all supplies can be viewed at any time and are ready at hand. Yet another highlight, this time from the systemat product line: the SLD pull-out system. The so-called "SlimLine Drawer" narrow frame gives the pull-out a particularly large capacity and produces an interesting filigree design. Intelligent opening systems for handleless fronts ensure uncomplicated handling – whether mechanically or electronically.
