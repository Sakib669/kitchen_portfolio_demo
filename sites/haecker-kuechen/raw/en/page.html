Häcker Kitchens – Custom Designs for Your Home

We have a mission: We want to bring our customers’ kitchen visions to life worldwide—down to the very last detail. With exclusive designs, high-quality craftsmanship, and smart solutions, we create kitchens that impress with both design and functionality. Immerse yourself in our diverse selection of styles, materials, and colors, and let our passion for quality and functionality inspire you. With Häcker Kitchens, your kitchen becomes the cozy heart of your home—tailored precisely to your needs and preferences. Discover the world of Häcker: a universe full of inspiring possibilities.

Get inspired now 

A Kitchen Designed Just for You

With a wide range of design possibilities and customization options, Häcker offers you the opportunity to tailor your built-in kitchen perfectly to your wishes and needs—from the selection of colors and cabinet front designs to the layout and the interior configuration of the cabinets and pull-outs. Our two product lines, concept130 and systemat, offer you a wide range of possibilities, perfectly complemented by our high-quality selection of accessories. Quality, functionality, and aesthetics come together in perfect harmony to highlight your personal style and enrich your home with a kitchen from Häcker. Whether as the center of family life, a place for culinary adventures, or a retreat for moments of reflection: only when the kitchen reflects your way of life does it become what it’s meant to be—a custom-designed space for countless beautiful moments and experiences. Discover more about our various kitchen designs with our exclusive online kitchen planner and be inspired by our high-quality full-extension drawers, modern cabinet front designs, and captivating lighting arrangements.

In our kitchens, people come first

Häcker is an owner-managed family business that understands the importance of a well-designed kitchen. That’s how we meet the demands of modern life—from exquisite design and proven quality to multifunctional, customized layouts. Our kitchens offer smart solutions and well-thought-out features to make your kitchen the well-equipped heart of your home. From smart storage solutions to the ergonomic design of the workspace—our custom kitchens are carefully designed and tested during the planning phase to make your time in the kitchen more efficient and enjoyable. Together with our employees and partners, we’re constantly working to further refine our kitchens for you.

A Career at Häcker Küchen

To meet our high standards for kitchen quality, we are always looking for new Häcker team members who are eager to contribute to our internationally connected family business with enthusiasm and dedication. When communicating with our employees, we place a high priority on transparency, honesty, and reliability, as this is the only way to foster a trusting working relationship. Häcker is also deeply committed to offering employees the highest level of flexibility. After all, it is only through flexible solutions in the way we organize work that we can attract satisfied employees who are committed to and enjoy contributing to Häcker—a central cornerstone of our corporate culture. Numerous benefits, as well as opportunities for professional development and training, demonstrate that the well-being of our employees is our top priority. Because we know: Only when everyone pulls together can we create top-quality kitchens—day in and day out.

Quality starts with manufacturing

Certified quality and continuous improvement for your kitchen: This commitment forms the foundation of our Häcker quality policy. Our quality management system, certified by DMSZ (German Management System Certification Association) in accordance with ISO 9001, ensures that our products deliver on our quality promise in terms of durability, resilience, functionality, and safety. Every detail is crafted with precision and dedication. Our in-house testing laboratory subjects our materials to regular inspections to ensure their resistance to water vapor, humidity, and fluctuating climatic conditions. Both internal and external quality controls guarantee that our furniture meets defined quality standards. Whether you choose natural wood or high-gloss lacquered surfaces for your kitchen—thanks to internal and external inspections, every Häcker product meets verified standards for quality and design. With Häcker, you can create a space for togetherness, cooking, and shared experiences. 

Find the Perfect Kitchen 

Do you dream of a colorful kitchen?

You can never go wrong with these: Classic kitchen colors that remain consistently popular include bright white, subtle gray, and elegant black. But it can also be worth taking a chance on color. After all, the moods that can be created with different shades are just as diverse as our color palette. Our product lines, systemat and concept130, open up a wide range of possibilities here. How about a kitchen in fresh eucalyptus or warm emerald green? High-contrast color combinations or a palette of delicate cream tones are also possible. Or perhaps you’re in the mood for a vibrant, cheerful “Color Kitchen”? Whatever your preference may be: you decide, we deliver. It’s that simple.
