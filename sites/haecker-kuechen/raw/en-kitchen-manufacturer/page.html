Häcker - a company with tradition and a vision for the future

Our history

The foundation for Häcker's corporate history was laid back in 1898 when Hermann Häcker founded a joinery in Rödinghausen. His passion for quality craftsmanship and his attention to detail have been passed down from generation to generation ever since. In 1938, Friedrich Häcker took over the management until 1965, when his son-in-law Horst Finkemeier took over the joinery. Together with his wife Renate, he laid the foundation for today's economic success through the series production of kitchen furniture. In 1996, his son, Jochen Finkemeier, joined the management. Since then, a lot has happened in terms of innovation: from the introduction of the systemat and concept130 product lines to the construction of factories 3, 4 and 5 and the introduction of BLAUPUNKT as the exclusive own brand for built-in kitchen appliances. Häcker is proud to be a traditional, owner-managed family business that makes the kitchen dreams of many people around the world come true with heart and soul and expertise.

Our philosophy

Häcker has a mission: we want to make the kitchen dreams of customers all over the world come true. Therefore, the customer with his individual ideas, wishes and needs is the focus of our corporate philosophy in every respect. Our goal is to produce high-quality and noble kitchens that make your everyday life easier and at the same time become an inspiring place where you feel completely at ease - every day anew. 
Our work is characterised by attention to detail - from the perfectly smooth lacquered front to the environmentally friendly plinth base made from 100% recycled plastic. This is how we create unique products, because as a kitchen manufacturer with passion, we know that it's the little things that make the difference. Häcker maintains a close and trusting exchange with specialist dealers in order to optimally support and accompany you with our expertise in the implementation of individual customer wishes (/en/kuechenplaner). Because our mission is to create unique kitchen compositions that perfectly suit your lifestyle. This is how your kitchen becomes a special place - a place of life, creativity and encounter that deserves the highest level of quality and precision. 

Our Vision

Our vision drives us to continually strive for excellence and to push the boundaries of what is possible in kitchendesign. We want to remain among Germany’s leading kitchen manufacturers, actively shape the future of the kitchen industry, and have a positive impact on the lives of many people. This includes continuously improving our processes and strategies, as we are mindful of our responsibility—toward our partners, our employees, and society. To realize our vision, we rely on close collaboration with our customers, partners, and employees. We aim to create a trusting environment where ideas can be shared and great innovations can emerge. Together, we want to set new standards and drive groundbreaking innovations in the kitchen industry.

Our mission

As a renowned kitchen manufacturer, Häcker follows a philosophy that goes beyond the mere production of kitchens. Because we firmly believe that a kitchen is more than just a functional room. It is the centre of the home. A place where memories are made and family and friends come together. That's why we strive to offer our customers not only the best quality, but also maximum flexibility. So that you get a kitchen that perfectly matches your individual wishes and needs. In doing so, we not only want to do justice to current design trends, but also create timelessly beautiful and sustainable kitchens. In our products, we strive for the highest quality in every detail. Each of our fitted kitchens (/en/kueche-kaufen) combines functionality, technical sophistication and aesthetics. From the initial idea to the final implementation, we set the highest standards in terms of quality and precision to meet the highest expectations.

Our values

These three values form the foundation of our corporate philosophy: they are deliberately chosen adjectives, because they not only shape us but also define us. We are proud to be one of the best German kitchen manufacturers, producing not only high-quality kitchens but also taking our responsibility towards customers, partners, employees, the environment, and society seriously.

1. Reliable,

because trust is our foundation – and quality is our promise.

2. Responsible,

because we do not promise the future – we shape it. 

3. Exemplary,

because with openness, service, and genuine cooperation we set new standards.

Numbers, data, facts

With our 5 factories and a production area of 223,500 m², we have spacious and state-of-the-art production facilities where around 1,000 kitchens are manufactured with precision and efficiency every day. In addition, we have an administrative area of 25,000 m², which acts as the organisational heart of our company. A showroom area of 3,800 m² invites you to explore our innovative kitchen solutions live and experience our variety of designs, materials and compositions first-hand. With over 2,000 employees, we form a highly motivated team of talented designers, craftsmen and experts who, with their great commitment, make Häcker what it is every day: the place where kitchen dreams come true. 

Awards and certificates

Häcker is proud of its certificates and awards: These include the GS mark for tested safety, the Golden M of the Deutsche Gütergemeinschaft Möbel e.V. (DGM) for high quality, environmental and emission standards, as well as the official award as a climate-neutral company. In addition, we have the PEFC seal of approval, which confirms the origin of our wood products from sustainably managed forests, the RAL label "Made in Germany", which not only certifies the origin of our products, but also the fulfilment of quality criteria such as innovation, durability and healthy living. We also meet not only the new E05 emissions standard, but also the demanding U.S. Standards TSCA Title VI. These certificates confirm the commitment to quality, sustainability and safety that we have dedicated ourselves to as a kitchen manufacturer.
