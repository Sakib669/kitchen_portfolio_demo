concept130: Your kitchen that keeps pace with life

Häcker’s concept130 is designed for kitchens that do more than just cook. This is where people have breakfast and work, where homework is done, and where guests gather in the evening while the meal is being put together. 

concept130 is a true all-rounder: a modern product line with a wide range of design options, where cabinet fronts, colors, and finishes can be freely combined. Three cabinet heights let you customize the countertop to your exact measurements. What this versatility means in everyday life is evident in the people for whom a concept130 kitchen is designed. 

Experience Häcker in-store now 

What Your concept130 Kitchen Should Be Able to Do

A color you simply love: In a concept130 kitchen, it flows seamlessly from the cabinet front, over the handle and trim, all the way to the shelf. This creates a cohesive look even in small spaces, and tall cabinets maximize storage by utilizing the full height of the room. 

Saturday’s big grocery haul that needs to be stowed away all at once: Pull-outs with raised side rails offer more storage space and security. In the corner of the room, the pull-outs of the LeMans corner solution swing forward so that nothing gets lost in the back of the cabinet. 

Sunday, when several people are cooking at the same time: one is chopping, another is stirring, and someone else is plating up. The island gives everyone space; its rounded side panel softens the edge, and the round countertop is the perfect spot to gather for a coffee break. To see what a kitchen island with a granite-look front looks like, check out the concept130 kitchen island with a granite-look front; you’ll find planning tips in the magazine article on kitchen islands. 

Three moments, three very different kitchens—and all can be designed with concept130. The range spans from a color-rich kitchen in a compact space to understated Japandi design and modern country style, from urban industrial style to an open-concept kitchen-diner. 

The idea behind it is simple: The concept130 kitchen is tailored to the people who live in it—their habits, their space, and their taste. Häcker’s kitchenstyles demonstrate the variety of design directions that can be achieved with this system. 

Just Show More Personality

At the heart of concept130 is the combination. Cabinet fronts, colors, and finishes are coordinated and can be freely mixed and matched. This combination results in a kitchen that reflects your personal style. 

The selection of cabinet fronts ranges from smooth surfaces in matte lacquer or high-gloss to framed fronts in a modern country style, as well as wood finishes ranging from light oak to dark walnut. In addition, there are surfaces with a granite look, metallic tones, and polymer glass. Two cabinet fronts in one kitchen, wood alongside color, matte alongside glossy: the true creative freedom lies in the combination. 

And the color doesn’t stop at the cabinet fronts. Handles, trim, shelves, side panels, and base panels can all be designed in a cohesive color scheme, and shelves are also available in the same colors as the countertops. Molding is also available in the wood finishes Natural Walnut Repro and Natural Fine Oak Repro, for a wood look down to the last detail. The cabinet body also becomes a design element: The designer cabinet body in Light Gray, Titanium, Natural Fine Oak Repro, or Natural Walnut Repro adds its own accents, and visible sides are available in matte lacquer. 

A concept130 kitchen that combines a framed front in cashmere with a dark wood finish demonstrates how a mix of fronts can look. 

The Right Working Height for Your Back

How comfortable a kitchen is in everyday use depends on the work height. People come in all sizes, and a countertop that’s just right for one person may be too low or too high for another. 

That’s why concept130 is available in three cabinet heights: 71.5 cm, 78 cm, and 84.5 cm. This allows you to adjust the work height to your height. In everyday life, this means you can chop vegetables with a straight back, without having to bend over or hunch your shoulders. With tall cabinets standing 234 cm high, the kitchen makes full use of the vertical space as well. 

Added to this are design details that are barely noticeable at first glance but make all the difference in everyday life. Hidden mounting brackets and integrated ventilation systems for refrigerators ensure a clean, uncluttered look. The Metro pull-outs glide quietly and effortlessly, and interior organization featuring solid wood, cutlery inserts, or additional internal drawers quickly keeps things tidy. Glass cabinets can be configured with a narrow metal-framed door in stainless steel finishes with frosted glass or with a metal-framed door featuring concealed hinges; doors with 165-degree hinges open particularly wide. For corner spaces, Qanto offers a solution that rises at the push of a button, freeing up space for larger kitchen appliances—power supply included. 

You can try out different kitchen layouts using Häcker’s kitchenplanner. It’s best to determine the appropriate counter height together with your dealer.

Colors and Finishes

Solid colors ranging from crystal white to black, wood finishes from fine oak to walnut, matte lacquer, high-gloss, and laminate: concept130 offers a wide range of options. Lacquer has been part of the concept130 lineup from the very beginning. Countertops are available in all finishes in 25- or 40-millimeter thicknesses—slim for a sleek look or substantial for a bold edge. 

The Selection series provides bold accents with the latest trend colors. Fronts, handles, and trim can be coordinated in the same shade—even extending to visible sides, side panels, and shelves. This creates a cohesive look with a single color rather than isolated splashes of color. This also works with solid colors: side panels, shelves, and trim are available in matte lacquer in shades ranging from crystal white to black. The Selection colors are coordinated with natural and wood tones and pair well with wood finishes.

The surface itself also shapes the character. Laminate is resistant to scratches and impacts, while PerfectSense surfaces have a velvety feel. From understated Japandi design to modern country style with framed fronts, each of these styles can be translated into color and material. 

Simply Spend More Time at Home

The kitchen has long been more than just a place to cook. Additional elements create storage or workspaces—such as for a laptop—close to the action. The areas remain clearly separated from one another, so that cooking and working do not interfere with each other. 

Coordinated design solutions extend the kitchen into the living area. A sideboard in the dining area or a lowboard in the living room echoes the kitchen’s cabinet fronts and colors, creating a harmonious look between the kitchen and living area. The coat rack in the entryway can be customized in height, width, and layout so that jackets, shoes, and bags have their own designated spot right by the door. In the utility room, the kitchen and adjoining space appear to be a seamless whole. Here, short distances, efficient workflows, and plenty of storage space are key, and large, semi-open compartments accommodate the laundry baskets. A utility room in Crystal White shows what this looks like. 

The kitchen itself can also feel cozy. The SlightLift wall cabinet can be slid open and folded down; when closed, it’s a wall cabinet, and when open, it becomes a decorative shelf with atmospheric lighting. This creates a home where colors and materials flow seamlessly from room to room. Häcker’s kitchenfurniture showcases the perfect pieces to complement this design. 

Experience the concept130 at a specialty retailer

Colors, finishes, and cabinet heights look different in person than they do on a screen. The best way to find out how a cabinet front feels and what counter height works best for you is to try them out in person, with material samples in hand. 

During the planning consultation, all the elements that define your kitchen come together: the mix of cabinet fronts, the cohesive color scheme, the cabinet height, and the solutions for corners and storage space. A Häcker dealer near you can advise you on cabinet fronts, colors, and planning, and work with you to design a concept130 kitchen tailored to your everyday life.
