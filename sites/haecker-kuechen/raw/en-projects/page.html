Project-Based Business with Häcker Küchen – Your Partner for Kitchens of Any Size

From planning to installation: In our custom project business, our kitchens are tailored to your needs, reliable, and built to German standards.

Contact us now

Why Choose Häcker for Your Construction Project?

Comprehensive 5-Year Warranty

We offer a 5-year warranty as standard on all kitchen models and on Blaupunkt appliances. For Systema kitchens, you have the option to extend the warranty for an additional five years upon registration.

Over 1,000 kitchens per day

At its five German manufacturing plants, Häcker produces 1,100 kitchens every day: highly automated, yet customized. Modern production technology ensures precise and on-time completion.

Customized Solutions

A custom-made kitchen set? No problem. Häcker Küchen manufactures to order without keeping a stock of finished products. Choose your custom kitchen from over 200 billion possible combinations.

Quality Made in Germany

We design and manufacture all our kitchens at our two locations in Germany: The quality must meet the strict criteria of our in-house testing laboratory. 

International Experience

Häcker Kitchens Worldwide: Custom kitchens are installed in quantities ranging from as few as five to as many as over 1,000 on international construction projects.

Full-Service Project Management

From planning to installation: Häcker offers comprehensive project management for your large-scale construction project. 

Kitchen Project Planning: The Collaboration Process

1. Initial
Contact: The start of a service-oriented partnership! In every country, you’ll find a personal contact person who will discuss your initial questions and requirements with you. 

2. Clarifying
the Framework Clear coordination as the foundation: As experienced partners for kitchens in large-scale construction projects, we know how important seamless communication is. We ensure that misunderstandings don’t arise in the first place. 

3. Planning
: Precise project planning for custom kitchen solutions: In this step, we discuss all details regarding the kitchens. Whether you need many custom kitchens or kitchen series—we tailor our approach exactly to your specific requirements.

4. Manufacturing
High-quality production with consistent standards? A given for us! We implement what we’ve discussed professionally and with precision. Even when producing a large number of kitchens, we meet the highest quality standards.

5. Delivery:
Coordinated logistics according to the construction schedule: You can rely on us completely! We deliver our products right on time, exactly when you’re ready. 

6. Installation
We oversee professional installation by experienced teams so you can focus entirely on the rest of your project. This ensures our kitchens integrate seamlessly into your project.

Product Lines & Design Variety – Ideal for Your Construction Projects

Häcker Küchen develops kitchen solutions that combine functionality, design, and proven quality in a system that ensures reliable planning. With the systemat and concept130 product lines, we offer a consistent modular grid (130 mm) that enables efficient, flexible kitchen planning and precise integration into a wide variety of floor plan concepts. These two product lines position us in the mid-range to premium price segment.

For kitchen project planning, this means: a high degree of design freedom, customized solutions even in standard production, and a wide range of configuration options. In addition, our living-room-ready furniture—such as sideboards, tables, and storage solutions—creates a harmonious connection between the kitchen and living areas. The minimalist design language blends seamlessly into modern architectural and interior design concepts.
 

concept130: Well-designed and flexible

The concept130 product line stands out for its high level of planning flexibility and a design philosophy consistently focused on user needs. Thanks to a holistic color concept for fronts, cabinet bodies, and trim, versatile, custom-tailored kitchen solutions can be created.

With eleven exterior cabinet colors and a uniform interior color—Crystal White—concept130 enables a harmonious, cohesive design—from planning through to final installation. A distinctive design feature is the Selection color palette, which is regularly updated to reflect current color and interior design trends.
 

systemat: Premium Kitchens with Design Freedom

With its “systemat” product line, Häcker Küchen offers a high-quality solution for sophisticated kitchen and living space designs. The line is characterized by a wide variety of design options, such as elegant wood and stone veneer fronts and three different cabinet heights.

The combination of precise craftsmanship, distinctive materials, and clean lines makes “systemat” the ideal choice for style-conscious living spaces. With over 1,900 NCS and 190 RAL colors, virtually any color vision can be brought to life.
 

High-quality technology from Blaupunkt

For over a decade, there has been a close strategic partnership between Häcker Küchen and the Blaupunkt brand. The goal of this collaboration is to offer comprehensive kitchen solutions in which cabinetry and kitchen appliances are perfectly coordinated—in design, functionality, and quality.

Blaupunkt built-in appliances, sinks, and faucets are available exclusively in combination with a Häcker kitchen. This brand partnership ensures that project clients receive particularly reliable appliance solutions—tailored to the requirements of large-scale construction projects.
 

In-House Exhibition

At its Rödinghausen location, Häcker Küchen offers an extensive showroom spanning over 4,000 m² for viewing samples and obtaining product information. More than 40 kitchen and living solutions are on display to inspire project kitchens: exemplary designs from the systemat and concept130 lines, which are characterized by high planning flexibility, functional depth, and design versatility. 

The concepts on display illustrate a variety of living and usage scenarios—from urban micro-living to premium solutions—and are ideally suited as a planning basis for large-scale construction projects. The portfolio is complemented by furniture components suitable for living spaces, such as sideboards, tables, and dressing systems, which can be integrated into a wide variety of interiors thanks to their minimalist design.

Certified Quality for Your Construction Project

We are also a reliable kitchen manufacturer with proven product quality for our partners in the project business. Our kitchens carry recognized certifications—including the RAL quality mark and TÜV certifications—that confirm specific requirements for material selection and workmanship. When you choose Häcker Kitchens, you’re choosing products whose quality has been independently tested and documented. In this way, we offer you a partnership built on trust and proven product safety.

Frequently Asked Questions About Project Business

What is a custom kitchen?

A project kitchen is a standardized, preconfigured kitchen designed for large-scale construction and real estate projects, such as apartment buildings, vacation resorts, or business apartments. The kitchen manufacturer designs, manufactures, and installs project kitchens in large quantities. Developers and architects value the optimal balance between quality, functionality, and cost-effectiveness.

How does project planning for kitchens work in large-scale projects?

Our first contact marks the beginning of a reliable and service-oriented partnership. Once all project parameters have been agreed upon, we develop a detailed design for custom kitchen solutions. The subsequent production at our local facilities is based on state-of-the-art manufacturing technology. We guarantee consistently high standards. Timely delivery in accordance with the construction schedule, along with precise installation by qualified installation teams, ensure the successful completion of the project.

At what order quantity does it become worthwhile to take on a project?

That depends on many different factors: We've even completed projects with as many as five kitchens. Contact us to get an assessment of your project's cost-effectiveness!

How do delivery and installation work?

Delivery and installation are carried out according to the agreed-upon construction schedule. Just-in-time production, on-time deliveries, and professional installation are all part of our commitment to quality.
